Ancla verifier

Check a Costa Rican procurement record against a Merkle root committed to DecentralChain. All verification runs in this page. Nothing is sent anywhere except a read-only request to the public node.

Proof

Anchor account (optional)

A passing check proves the record matches the root that was committed, and that the commitment happened when the chain says it did. It does not prove the record is accurate, and it says nothing about anything that happened before the first anchor. Anchoring establishes integrity forward from the moment it starts.
