This recomputes the bundle digest from the manifest and compares it with the commitment on chain. A match proves the manifest was not edited after it was published: the counts, both archive hashes and both Merkle roots are all inside the digest. It does not prove the changes file is the one those two archives produce. Only rebuilding from the archives does that, and only ancla verify-bundle can, because it needs both files.

A passing check proves the record matches the root that was committed, and that the commitment happened when the chain says it did. It does not prove the record is accurate, and it says nothing about anything that happened before the first anchor. Anchoring establishes integrity forward from the moment it starts.
